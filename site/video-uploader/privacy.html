Privacy Policy

Video Uploader. Effective October 23, 2026.

Video Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the What I Meant to Say YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. This policy explains what Google user data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Data it accesses

With the channel owner's authorization, granted on Google's sign-in page, Video Uploader uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails, and captions to the authorized channel, together with the titles, descriptions, tags, and privacy settings the owner chose;
  • read the authorized channel's name and ID, to confirm where uploads will go.

It does not read anyone else's data, and it does not access viewers, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other Google service.

How it is stored

The authorization token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner's account. Video files and their details stay on that computer until they are uploaded to YouTube. Video Uploader has no servers, no website accounts, no cookies, and no analytics.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI or machine learning model.

Video Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

Video Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (also reachable from Third-party apps and services in your Google Account). Revoking access invalidates the stored token; the owner can also delete the token file from their computer. Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube until removed there.

Changes and contact

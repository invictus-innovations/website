Video Uploader

Video Uploader is a private tool that the owner of the What I Meant to Say YouTube channel uses to publish that channel's own music videos. It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files to the What I Meant to Say channel.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, privacy setting, custom thumbnail, and captions.
  • Reads the authorized channel's name to confirm uploads go to the right channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API with access that the channel owner grants through Google's own sign-in page, and the owner can revoke that access at any time.
